About Me

Not just code —
it's a craft.

I'm Divyansh, a Full Stack Developer from Kangra, Himachal Pradesh, currently in my first year of BCA. I enjoy turning ideas into real websites, web applications and digital products that people can actually use.

I work across both frontend and backend development, from designing clean user interfaces to building APIs, databases, authentication systems and deployment workflows.

Most of what I build starts with a simple question: How can this idea become something useful? That's what drives me to keep learning, experimenting and improving with every project.

Outside development, I create Minecraft cinematic videos and storytelling content. It has taught me a lot about visual design, pacing, creativity and attention to detail — skills that also influence the way I build websites.
