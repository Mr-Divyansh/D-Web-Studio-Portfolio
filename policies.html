Privacy, terms & policies

What happens when you browse this site, send an enquiry, or discuss a project.

Privacy notice

This notice describes the information handled by the D Web Studio website. The site presents Divyansh's development work and provides ways to make a project enquiry.

Information you provide

The contact form requires your name, email address and message. Phone/WhatsApp, company/business, project type and budget are optional. Only the fields you complete are submitted.

How an enquiry is handled

When you submit the form, its contents are sent to Web3Forms at api.web3forms.com and routed to the studio inbox. The information is used to read and respond to your enquiry and discuss a possible project. Web3Forms processes submissions under its own Privacy Policy and Terms.

The form does not define a fixed retention period. Enquiry copies may remain in the provider's systems and the inbox used to receive them; their retention and deletion behavior depends on those services and their settings. For a request about an enquiry you sent to the studio, email dwebstudio00@gmail.com.

If you contact the studio by email, WhatsApp or a social account instead, the message is handled through that service and its own privacy practices also apply.

External services and site measurement

Pages load fonts and interface libraries from Google Fonts, cdnjs and unpkg. Your browser makes requests to those providers, which may process technical connection data under their own policies: Google, Cloudflare and npm. The site's source does not include analytics or advertising-tracking code.

Cookie and local storage notice

The site stores your light, dark or system theme choice in browser localStorage so that your appearance preference can persist. The site's own code does not use analytics or advertising cookies. External providers loaded by the site may use their own technologies; consult their privacy information and your browser settings for details. No consent for analytics or advertising cookies is requested because the site source contains no such tracking.

Website terms

Portfolio entries and service descriptions are provided to help visitors understand the work and discuss a potential project. A particular project's deliverables, schedule, fee, payment milestones, ownership/licensing, support and cancellation terms should be agreed in a written proposal or agreement before work begins. This website does not set those project-specific terms.

Links to third-party websites and services are provided for convenience. Those services are operated by others and have their own terms and privacy practices.

Project payments and refunds

This website does not offer online checkout or sell a digital product. Projects are discussed individually and should have their payment schedule and any cancellation or refund terms set out in the written project agreement before payment. The applicable agreement and law govern a particular project; this notice does not promise a blanket refund or remove any rights that cannot legally be waived.

If a proposal does not explain a payment, cancellation or refund term, ask for it in writing before paying.

Questions about these notices? Email D Web Studio.
