# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JavaScript (existing codebase)

## Users

- Prospective clients, including businesses and creators, evaluating a developer for websites, web apps, and related digital work.
- Visitors reviewing Divyansh's work, experience, service offerings, and contact options.

## Product Purpose

D Web Studio is a portfolio and service website presenting Divyansh Kumar's development work, experience, services, and contact options. Its purpose is to help prospective clients evaluate the work and start an enquiry.

The intended success outcome is a qualified client enquiry. The site also needs to communicate the developer's capabilities accurately and make the portfolio easy to review.

## Positioning

The site presents D Web Studio as a solo developer-led web studio offering design and development for websites, landing pages, and web applications. Its current positioning combines portfolio examples with service information and direct contact paths.

## Operating Context

- The site is used by prospective clients who want to review work and services before contacting the studio.
- Visitors can browse project details, service descriptions, experience information, social profiles, and contact options.
- The site is a static, multi-page website intended for desktop and mobile browsers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static multi-page site with portfolio, project detail, about, services, experience, work, and contact pages.
- Theme preference controls for light, dark, and system appearance.
- Responsive layouts and mobile navigation.
- The current implementation uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript without an application framework; preserve this structure unless a deliberate migration is requested.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: D Web Studio
- The site identifies Divyansh Kumar as its developer and uses the D Web Studio name and logo.
- Product voice should remain professional, approachable, and technically credible.
- Explicit presentation constraint: the site should feel human-crafted rather than generic or obviously AI-templated.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portfolio entries, project pages, service descriptions, experience content, and contact options are present in the repository.
- The studio logo and project imagery are stored in the `img` directory.
- Site structure and behavior are implemented in `index.html`, `about.html`, `services.html`, `experience.html`, `work.html`, `contact.html`, and related CSS/JS files.
- Do not invent client names, testimonials, results, or other proof that is not present in the repository.

## Product Principles

1. Make capabilities, services, and project evidence easy for prospective clients to evaluate.
2. Keep service and project claims faithful to the available evidence.
3. Make it straightforward for interested visitors to contact the studio.
4. Balance technical credibility with clear, approachable communication.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- The current site includes keyboard-visible focus styling, skip navigation, and responsive navigation.
- Preserve readable contrast and keyboard operation across both themes.
- Avoid decorative-only controls and interactions that obstruct navigation or content access.
