How long does it take to build a website?

Landing pages usually take 3–5 days, business websites around 7–10 days. Web applications depend on the features — you get a clear timeline with the quote itself.

How much will my project cost?

Landing pages start at ₹4,999 and business websites at ₹9,999. After you share your requirement, you get an exact quote — no hidden costs later.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business details, what you want the website to do, and any logo, photos or content you already have. Don't worry if you don't have everything — I'll guide you.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes — every website is built mobile-first, so it looks and works perfectly on phones, tablets and desktops. Most of your customers will visit from a phone.

What if I need changes after the website is live?

Small changes and content updates are easy — just message me. Support doesn't end at launch.
