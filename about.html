About Me

Hi, I'm Divyansh — I turn ideas into working software.

Frontend developer, freelancer, and someone who's happiest when something he built actually works — for a client, or just for himself.

My Story

From curiosity to craft.

I'm currently a BCA student (Semester 1), based in Kangra, Himachal Pradesh — and alongside my studies, I've been working as a freelance frontend developer, building websites for real businesses through Fiverr.

Client work taught me how to build fast and reliably under a deadline. But the projects that taught me the most are the ones nobody paid me for — AttendX, Shadow-Weaver, and CHIKA started as "I wonder if I can build this," and turned into full working products with real users in mind.

I care about the same three things on every project, big or small: does it look like it was made with intent, does it actually work everywhere, and would I be proud to put my name on it.
