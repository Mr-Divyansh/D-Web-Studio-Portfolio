My Journey

From curiosity in school to real products.

How I went from discovering code in 9th grade to building websites, AI-powered systems and freelance projects — and what that path means for the work I do today.

The Journey

Every stage taught me something.

No shortcuts and no overnight success — just years of small experiments that slowly turned into real skills.

  1. Stage 01 · 9th & 10th Grade

    It started with one question: how is this made?

    My journey into technology started back in 9th and 10th grade, when I first discovered coding and became curious about how websites, applications and software were created. At first, coding was simply something interesting to explore — but over time, that curiosity became a serious passion.

    I started experimenting with different programming concepts, building small projects and understanding how websites actually work. That is when I realised that technology can turn an idea into something real.

    First lines of code Curiosity Small experiments
  2. Stage 02 · 11th & 12th Grade

    Theory was no longer enough.

    During 11th and 12th grade, I started taking coding much more seriously. Instead of only learning from theory, I focused on actually writing code — building projects, solving problems and exploring different areas of development.

    I spent a lot of time strengthening my programming fundamentals and learning web development, while experimenting with different technologies and tools. Every project gave me something new to learn, and every mistake helped me understand development better.

    Fundamentals Web development Problem solving
  3. Stage 03 · After School

    One gap year, fully dedicated to building.

    After completing school, I decided to take a gap year and use that time to focus completely on learning and building. That year became an important part of my journey, because I was able to explore technology far beyond what I had learned before.

    I worked on different websites and applications, experimented with modern development tools, and started exploring Artificial Intelligence. I became interested in how AI could be integrated into real applications instead of simply being used as a chatbot.

    Deep practice Modern tooling First AI experiments
  4. Stage 04 · AI, Workflows & Automation

    Solving problems with systems, not just code.

    As I explored AI further, I started learning how AI-powered systems, workflows and automations can be created to solve practical problems. I experimented with AI tools, automation workflows, APIs and different ways of connecting technologies together.

    This helped me understand that development is not only about writing code — it is also about finding smarter ways to solve problems and creating systems that can actually be useful.

    AI integrations Automation APIs
  5. Stage 05 · Personal Projects

    Learning by building — even when it breaks.

    During this period, I also started working on personal projects and experimenting with different ideas. Some projects were successful, while others were simply experiments that taught me what not to do.

    I explored frontend development, backend concepts, databases, APIs, AI integrations, automation, deployment and modern development workflows. Building things myself helped me understand technology much more deeply than simply watching tutorials.

    Frontend Backend Databases Deployment
  6. Stage 06 · Freelancing

    From practice projects to a real-world mindset.

    I also started exploring freelancing and working on projects with a real-world mindset. Instead of building projects only for practice, I began thinking about how websites and digital products could help businesses, creators and individuals.

    This pushed me to improve not only my technical skills, but also my understanding of design, user experience and communication — and how a digital product should actually solve a client's problem.

    Real clients Design & UX Communication
  7. Stage 07 · Today

    BCA, first year at Sri Sai University.

    Today, I am pursuing my BCA in my first year at Sri Sai University. College has given me a new environment to strengthen my computer science fundamentals while continuing the practical learning I started on my own.

    I am currently exploring areas such as web development, AI, automation, software development and emerging technologies, while continuing to build projects and improve my skills.

    CS fundamentals Web + AI Still building
Where I Am Now

Still at the beginning — and that's the point.

I am still at the beginning of my professional journey, and I don't consider myself someone who has finished learning. Technology changes constantly, and that is one of the things I enjoy most about it.

There is always something new to understand, experiment with and build. My goal is to keep learning through real projects, take part in hackathons and challenges, work with different technologies, and gradually turn my skills into meaningful products and solutions.
